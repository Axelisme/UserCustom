# Migrate to Orchestrate v131

## From

v130

## Observable changes

The landing is a squash commit. The landing commit records the `Landed:` trailer, close-out removes task refs and branches, and candidate selection uses `integration candidate`.

## Active-task impact

No automatic conversion is performed. Coordinate any active v130 task manually before adopting the v131 landing lifecycle; Orchestrate does not rewrite or clean task state.

## Manual steps

1. Finish or pause in-flight landing work at a clean, known SHA.
2. Use `integration candidate` for candidate selection.
3. Confirm the final landing is one squash commit with the `Landed:` trailer.
4. Confirm close-out removes the task refs and branches only through the normal lifecycle.

## Verification

Inspect the landed commit and its `Landed:` trailer, then verify the task refs and branches were removed by the explicit close-out operation.

## Rollback

Stop before `pin set` and continue from the retained clean task SHA. Never delete or rewrite active task evidence to simulate adoption.
