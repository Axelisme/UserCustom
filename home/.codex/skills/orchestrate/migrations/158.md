# Migrate to Orchestrate v158

## The `lane check` payload is described where it is read

v156 added `contract_commits` to `lane check` and v157 added the gate-script warning, but the two
places a reader looks the command up were never revisited. `SKILL.md`'s observation section and
`dispatch.md`'s opening both still listed `sha`, `base`, and `protected_paths` alone. Both now
describe the whole payload. No behaviour changed; the documentation caught up with two releases.

## Three restatements collapse

- `SKILL.md`'s authority sentence and `dispatch.md`'s `## Authority` both declared Root-ownership
  lists overlapping on collection, acceptance, landing, and recovery. `SKILL.md` now opens with
  "Beyond the shared authority in dispatch.md" and names only its own additions — the same idiom
  v156 applied to `runtime-pi.md`, now used by all three.
- `dispatch.md` restated the validation-mode selection criteria that `SKILL.md`'s lifecycle owns.
  It keeps the part a dispatch contract needs — Root chooses at admission, a worker cannot
  downgrade TDD — and points at the lifecycle for the criteria.
- `dispatch.md` named the **identity binding** and then re-spelled its five fields three lines
  later; the three `lane-worker` ports spelled them out without using the term at all. All four
  sites now use it. `SKILL.md` also used "envelope" for the same bundle, a third sense beside
  dev-flow's frozen `Envelope` and the one v156 renamed.

## Preserved mechanisms

Text only. No CLI grammar, payload key, ref, trailer, exit code, or lifecycle step changes.
